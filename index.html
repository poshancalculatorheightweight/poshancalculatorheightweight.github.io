    Poshan Calculator 2026: Height Weight Growth Tracker Guide

    The Poshan Calculator is a specialized online tool built to evaluate child growth standards using height, weight, and age data. Designed for parents, Anganwadi workers, and healthcare supervisors, this Poshan Calculator height weight app simplifies monitoring child health according to updated WHO growth indicators.

    Key Features of Poshan Tracker Calculator

    • Instant Malnutrition Screening: Categorizes growth metrics into Normal, Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM), and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM).
    • Custom Diet Recommendations: Provides tailored diet charts to combat child stunting and underweight conditions.
    • Mobile Friendly: Fast single-page architecture optimized for low-bandwidth mobile devices.
